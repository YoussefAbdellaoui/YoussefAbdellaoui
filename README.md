## 👨‍💻 About me

I like turning ideas into products people can actually use.

I work professionally in software engineering and spend a lot of my time outside of work designing, building, and shipping my own products — from web platforms to native iOS applications.

But code is only one way I like to create.

I'm also a photographer and filmmaker. I share my photography with the Unsplash community and document the places I visit through **The World Through My Lens**.

- 💻 Building products across **web and mobile**
- 📱 Shipping native **iOS applications**
- 📸 **642K+ views** and **61K+ downloads** on Unsplash
- 🌏 Documented a **47-day journey across 7 Asian countries**
- 🎥 Published **46 travel videos** from that journey
- 🌍 I speak **Spanish, English, German, and Arabic**

> Whether it's software, photography, or film, I mostly just enjoy making things.
